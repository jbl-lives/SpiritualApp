What is African Spirituality

African Spirituality is a rich and deeply rooted belief system that focuses on the connection between the living, ancestors, nature, and the divine. You the healer are a spiritual guide, and your key role is to maintain balance, offering insight, healing, and connecting to the spiritual world. Understanding your gift requires embracing the idea that the physical and spiritual worlds are intertwined, with ancestors and nature being central to maintaining the harmony of life.
